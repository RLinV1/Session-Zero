Down, warmth, rest

At 0 HP: Unconscious, roll for Frost each turn. Haul Out: an Action and Lore 8, up with 1 HP.

Warmth (the party's token): 1 a watch outside, 2 in a squall or whiteout, 1 a short rest. At 0: Snag on everything, 1d6 cold a watch.

Short rest: an hour, half your HP back, your Stones. Long rest: the hospice or the guildhouse.

On the board

  • Roll named attacks from the Play tab; the GM rules the hit.
  • Drag your token: the ruler counts squares, double on Difficult ground.
  • You see what your token sees. The GM sets the sight range to the weather.
