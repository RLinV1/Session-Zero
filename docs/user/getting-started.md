# Getting Started

## Joining a game as a player

1. Open the invite link the GM sent you, or scan the QR code they show.
2. Enter the name the table should see, and join.
3. Wait for the GM to give you a token. Until then you may see the map, or a dark board if
   the GM hides the map from players without a token.

You don't need an account. Your seat is saved in this browser, so closing the tab or
reloading brings you back as the same player. If you sign in, your seat is also kept on your
account, so you can open it on another device.

> **Same computer as the GM?** Open the invite link in a private window. A second tab in
> the GM's window is still the GM.

## Hosting your first game

### With an account

1. Sign up or sign in. You land on **Your rooms**.
2. Choose **Create room**, give it a name, and pick a game (the game sets up stats and
   conditions for that system).
3. In the room, add a map: upload one, or choose one from your library or the built-in maps.
4. Choose **Share** in the top corner and send the invite link or QR code to your players.

### Without an account

From the home page you can start a guest room on the built-in maps. A guest room works like
any other, but it can only use built-in art. It is removed after 30 days without play.
Create a free account to keep it: the room moves into your account.

### Try it first

The home page has an interactive tutorial with a short scripted fight. Each step has a
**Show me** button that demonstrates the control on the board.

## Finding your way around a room

| Part of the screen | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **Board** (centre) | The map and tokens. Drag empty space to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom, double-click to ping a spot for everyone. |
| **Tool rail** (left of the board) | Select, Measure, Draw, AoE, Eraser, and for the GM Fog, Terrain and Doors. See [Board tools](board-tools.md). |
| **Room bar** (top) | Room name, who's here, **Share** (GM), **Settings**, **More** (activity log, replay, guide), **Leave table**. |
| **Sidebar** (right) | Tabs: **Play**, **Tokens**, **Dice**, and **Manage** for the GM. Drag its edge to resize it, or hide it to give the board the whole screen. |
| **Turn banner** (top of the board) | During an encounter: whose turn it is, the round, and **End turn** for that token's owner. Shows on your own tokens' turns, or on every turn with **Settings → Preferences → Turn banner on every turn**. |

| Sidebar tab | GM | Player |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Play** | Rulings waiting on you, tokens you control, Attack, Initiative | Your tokens, Attack, the turn order |
| **Tokens** | Every token: search, find on map, edit, add new tokens | Every token you can see; edit your own |
| **Dice** | Roll dice, roll privately, see every roll, change a ruling | Roll dice and see every public roll |
| **Manage** | The map, grid, scenes and players who have left | (not shown) |

**More → Guide** walks you through these parts again at any time.
