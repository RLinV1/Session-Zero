# Settings

Open **Settings** from its button in the room bar. Audio, Graphics, Preferences and Language
are saved in your browser and apply only to you. The **Table** tab is the GM's and is shared
by everyone at the table.

## Audio

| Option | Does |
| --- | --- |
| Music volume | Volume of background music |
| Sound effects volume | Volume of dice, attack and board sounds |

## Graphics

| Option | Choices | Does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Graphics quality | High, Balanced, Low | Lower settings help slower devices |
| Reduced animations | On / off | Still effects instead of moving ones |
| Focus on attacks | On / off | Zooms the board to the fight when an attack plays |
| Attack animations | All, Current turn, None | Which attack animations play on your board. **Current turn** plays only the active token's. |

## Preferences

| Option | Does |
| --- | --- |
| Arrow keys move selected token | Move your selected token one square per key press |
| Turn banner on every turn (GM) | Shows the turn banner with **End turn** on every token's turn, not only yours |

## Table (GM, shared)

| Option | Choices | Does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Remove tokens at 0 HP | All, Only NPC tokens, Off | Removes tokens when their HP reaches 0. **Only NPC tokens** removes only tokens no player controls. With **Off**, downed tokens are marked on the board and stop blocking their square. Undo brings a removed token back. |
| Hide map from players without a token | On / off | Players who control no visible token see a dark board, with no tokens, areas or doors |
| Hide HP from Players | On / off | Players don't see HP of tokens they don't control |
| Hide AC from Players | On / off | Players don't see AC of tokens they don't control |
| Diagonals | Equal cost, Alternating (1, 2), Euclidean | How diagonal steps are costed, for drags and the ruler |
| Difficult terrain applies | On entry, On exit, Both | Whether a step costs double when it enters Difficult terrain, leaves it, or either |

Changing a table rule updates every player's board at once. Set your usual choices once in
**Room settings** on the dashboard, and every new room starts with them.

## Language

English or Traditional Chinese (繁體中文). The site switches, including messages from the
server. The AI build screens are still in English only.
