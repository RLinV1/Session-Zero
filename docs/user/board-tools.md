# Board Tools

The tool rail sits beside the board. Each tool shows a hint at the bottom of the board while
it's active. Press **Esc** to stop using a tool. Right-click also cancels most tools.

## Everyone

| Tool | What it does | How |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Select** | Move tokens you control, pan and zoom | Drag a token to move it. Drag empty space to pan. Scroll or pinch to zoom. Double-click to ping. Hold Alt to place freely. |
| **Measure** | Measure distance. Only you see it. | **Range** mode: drag for a straight line. **Movement** mode: click to add points, Enter to finish, Backspace removes a point. Hold Alt for a straight line. |
| **Draw** | Draw lines, rectangles, circles and freehand strokes everyone sees | Drag to draw. Choose the shape and colour in the tool's options. |
| **AoE** | Place a spell area everyone sees: circle, cone, line or box | Click to place a 2-square area, or drag to size and aim it; the size shows as you drag. Lines have a width setting. Select a placed area to move, resize or turn it by its handles. |
| **Eraser** | Erase your own marks, drawings and areas | Click or drag over them. **Clear** removes all your marks at once. |

## GM only

| Tool | What it does | How |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Fog** | Hide parts of the map from players | **Fog rectangle**: drag. **Fog polygon**: click corners, then click the first corner or press Enter. **Reveal**: click fog to remove it. |
| **Terrain** | Paint Difficult or Blocked terrain, or erase it | Choose a brush or rectangle and a paint (Normal erases, Difficult, Blocked), then drag. |
| **Doors** | Change doors and windows | Choose what a click does: **Open / close** or **Lock / unlock**, then click a door or window. |
| **Sight** | Turn line of sight on or off for the table | Toggle on the rail. |
| AoE **GM only** | Keep an area hidden from players | Tick **GM only** in the AoE options before placing. Hidden areas are hatched on your screen. |

## Board options

The board's options button holds **Fit map to screen** and, for the GM, the **Show** menu.
The Show menu chooses what your own view draws: Fog, Walls, Doors and windows, Terrain, Grid
and Token view. Terrain is on by default. These choices change only what you see, not what
players see.

## Board colours

| Feature | Looks like |
| --- | --- |
| Walls (GM view) | Thin green lines |
| Doors and windows | Their own colours, distinct from walls |
| Difficult terrain | Outlined, hatched area |
| Blocked terrain | Red, crosshatched area |
| Downed token (0 HP, when tokens aren't removed) | Marked on the board |
| Your fog (GM) and players' fog | Painted fog with a soft edge on the fogged side |

## Keyboard

| Key | Does |
| --- | --- |
| **Esc** | Stop the current tool or cancel an edit |
| **Arrow keys** | Move the selected token one square (when turned on in Settings → Preferences), or move the placement cursor |
| **Enter** | Place a token while placing, finish a route or polygon |
| **Backspace** | Remove the last point of a route or polygon |
| **Alt** (hold) | Place or measure freely, off the grid |
