# Troubleshooting and FAQ

## Connection

**The room says "Reconnecting…".** Your connection dropped. The room reconnects on its own
and catches up on everything you missed. Nothing you saw before is lost.

**"Open in too many places."** Your seat is open in too many tabs or devices. Close the ones
you aren't using, then reload.

**"Something went wrong" in a room.** Part of the page failed to draw. Choose **Reload**. If
it keeps happening, choose **Back to home** and tell the GM; the room itself is safe.

## Seats and access

**I opened the invite link again and became a new player.** Your seat is saved in the
browser you first joined from. Use that browser, or sign in: a signed-in player's seat is kept
on their account and can be opened on any device.

**"No access to this room."** The GM may have removed you, reset the invite link, or deleted
the room. Ask the GM for a new link.

**The link doesn't let anyone in.** The GM has locked the room. Players already seated can
still come back.

**I'm the GM and opened the invite link to test it, but I'm still the GM.** Your browser
remembers you. Open the link in a private window to join as a player.

## On the board

**My board is dark.** The GM has hidden the map from players without a token. You'll see the
board once the GM gives you one.

**I can't move my token there.** The path may be blocked by a wall, a closed door or Blocked
terrain, or another token may be standing in that square. The board shows why while you drag.

**I can't move a token at all.** Players can move only the tokens they control. Ask the GM to
add you under **Controlled by**.

**I don't see HP or AC on other tokens.** The GM has hidden them with a table rule.

**Grid or wall detection isn't available.** Detection needs the map analysis service, which a
self-hosted copy may not run. Set the grid by hand in the map editor and draw walls yourself.

## Accounts

**Do players need an account?** No. Only hosting with your own uploaded art needs one. Anyone
can host a guest room on the built-in maps.

**What happens to a guest room?** It's removed after 30 days without play. Create a free
account to keep it.
