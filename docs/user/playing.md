# Playing (Player Guide)

## Your tokens

The GM gives you control of one or more tokens. They appear under **My tokens** on the
**Play** tab. Click a name to find the token on the map.

You can edit your own tokens' HP and conditions from the **Tokens** tab. Depending on the
table's rules, you may not see other tokens' HP or AC.

## Moving

- **Drag** your token. With the grid on, the board shows the cheapest path to the square
  under the pointer, around walls, closed doors and Blocked terrain, with its cost. Difficult
  terrain costs double.
- **Arrow keys** move the selected token one square at a time (turn this on in
  **Settings → Preferences**).
- **Hold Alt** to place freely, off the grid.

You can pass through other tokens while dragging, but you can't end a snapped move on a
square another token is standing on. If the path is blocked, the board says so and the move
isn't made.

Other players see your token slide along the path it took.

## Seeing the board

With line of sight on, you see only what your tokens can see. Walls and closed doors block
sight; windows and open doors don't. Fog hides areas the GM hasn't revealed yet. If the GM
hides the map from players without a token, your board stays dark until you're given one.

## Your turn

When your token's turn comes up, a banner appears at the top of the room: "It's *name*'s
turn · Round *n*". When you're done, press **End turn** on the banner.

## Attacking

1. On the **Play** tab, under **Attack**, choose your target from the list, or **Pick target on
   board** and click the token.
2. Tap one of your saved attacks to roll it, or open **Custom roll** to build one.
3. The GM rules **Hit** or **Miss**. On a hit, roll damage; the GM applies it.

Targets out of your attack's range are listed separately and marked **Out of range**. You can
still choose them; the GM decides.

Your dice are thrown on everyone's board, and the attack's animation plays from your token to
the target.

## Rolling dice

On the **Dice** tab, build a roll: the number of dice, the die type, and **+** to add another
die type or a flat number (for example 1d8 + 2d6 + 3). Everyone sees public rolls. You can
also drag a die onto the map to throw it there.

## Measuring

Pick **Measure** on the tool rail:

- **Range** mode measures straight-line distance, ignoring terrain. Use it for spell ranges.
- **Movement** mode plans a route: click to add points, Enter to finish. It's costed the same
  way a drag is.

Only you see your measurements.

## Drawing and areas

- **Draw** leaves lines, shapes and freehand drawings everyone sees. You can erase your own.
- **AoE** places a spell area everyone sees. Click for a 2-square area, or drag to size and
  aim it; the size is shown as you drag. Select a placed area to move, resize or turn it.
- Double-click the map to **ping** a spot so everyone looks there.

## Chat

Write in the room chat, and type `@` to mention someone.
